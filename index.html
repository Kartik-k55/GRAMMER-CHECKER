Rule-Based NLP Modules in this Project

⚖️

Subject-Verb Agreement

Ensures singular subjects match 3rd-person singular verbs (e.g. He goes, not He go) and plural subjects match plural verbs.

⚙️

Verb Usage & Tenses

Checks bare infinitives after modals (can play), auxiliary do/did forms, and past participles after has/have/had.

🔤

Indefinite Articles

Applies phonetic vowel rules: an before vowel sounds (including silent h like an hour) and a before consonants.

🔁

Repeated Words

Flags accidental consecutive duplicate words (e.g. the the, to to) and eliminates redundancies.

✏️

Punctuation & Style

Verifies sentence capitalization, terminal punctuation (. ? !), uppercase pronoun I, and spacing.
